AMFI Scheme Code Design & Plan Economics

6-Digit Immutable Key: Unique identifier assigned by AMFI ensuring global uniqueness across schemes and plan variants.

SEBI Categorization Codes: Standardized mapping (e.g., EC01 for Large Cap, EC02 for Mid Cap, DC01 for Liquid).

Direct vs. Regular Spread: Regular plans average 1.35% TER vs. Direct plans at 0.78% TER.

Commission Delta: A 0.57% (57 bps) annual distributor drag compounds significantly over 10+ year investment horizons.

Quality Assurance

Data Quality Audit & Anomaly Detection

OBSERVATION 1

SIP YoY Growth Nulls

12 missing values in monthly_sip_inflows['yoy_growth_pct'] for months 2022-01 through 2022-12.

Domain Root Cause: Dataset begins in Jan 2022; calculating 1-year YoY percentage requires absent 2021 historical base.
OBSERVATION 2

Negative Drawdowns

All 40 schemes contain negative numbers in max_drawdown_pct (ranging from -2.23% to -33.5%).

Domain Context: Financial drawdown mathematically denotes peak-to-trough decline; negative signs are natively accurate.
OBSERVATION 3

KYC Compliance Split

In investor_transactions, 30,146 (91.97%) rows are Verified while 2,632 (8.03%) are Pending.

Workflow Handling: downstream settled AUM models must filter for Verified accounts to avoid provisional inflation.
